Lexiao Zou (邹乐骁) is a graduate student in the Department of Computer Science at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, supervised by [Prof. Miao Zhang](https://faculty.hitsz.edu.cn/zhangmiao). He received his bachelor's degree from the same institution. During his undergraduate studies, he interned at Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data (SRIBD), where he participated in the development of [Amphion](https://github.com/open-mmlab/Amphion) with [Prof. Zhizheng Wu](https://drwuz.com/).

His research centers on **data-centric AI**: improving AI systems through better data, supervision, and training dynamics rather than only scaling model architectures. Across recent work, this theme appears in diffusion-based dataset distillation, natural-language agent systems, and earlier audio / speech generation research.
