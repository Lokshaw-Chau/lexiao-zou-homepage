A more polished, AcadHomepage-inspired academic profile

Lexiao Zou is a graduate student in the Department of Computer Science at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, supervised by Prof. Miao Zhang. He obtained his bachelor's degree at the same institution. During his undergraduate study, he interned at Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data (SRIBD), where he participated in the development of Amphion with Prof. Zhizheng Wu.

His research interests lie in data-centric AI, with the long-term goal of understanding how better data, supervision, and model–data feedback loops can make intelligent systems evolve more effectively. Publicly visible work spans singing voice conversion, audio/music/speech generation, diffusion-based dataset distillation, and natural-language agents.
