Research

What I work on

I am interested in how data quality, model behavior, and evaluation protocols interact in modern AI systems. My recent work moves across dataset distillation, agent-oriented evaluation, and audio generation.

Dataset distillation

Efficient synthetic data construction and training curricula for stronger downstream learning.

Natural-language agents

Agent harnesses, evaluation setups, and practical system behavior under language-based interaction.

Audio & speech generation

Open-source generation pipelines, toolkits, and representation learning for speech and music.

Data-centric AI

Studying how better data and supervision design can improve capability, robustness, and efficiency.
