Hello! I am Lexiao Zou.

I am a graduate student in the Department of Computer Science at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Miao Zhang. I received my bachelor's degree from the same institution.

My research interests are centered around data-centric AI. I am broadly interested in how data quality, learning dynamics, and evaluation protocols shape capable AI systems. My recent work spans dataset distillation, natural-language agents, and audio/speech generation.

Short Bio

During my undergraduate study, I interned at Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data (SRIBD), where I participated in the development of Amphion with Prof. Zhizheng Wu. My recent research has gradually moved from audio generation and singing voice conversion toward broader questions in data quality, training dynamics, and AI system evaluation.
